What Spark Grid 1.0 includes

A free native Mac spreadsheet that opens and saves .xlsx and CSV on your machine, with everyday formulas and formatting, and without an account.

View on GitHub

macOS 14 or later · Free · No account · No analytics · The app does not use the network

What 1.0 does

  • New, Open, Save, and Save As for .xlsx and CSV. Autosave writes the file you already saved. Recent files. One window.
  • Selection, freeze panes, merge, fill handle, zoom, resize, and a formula bar that stays in sync with the cell.
  • Formulas that evaluate: SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, COUNTA, MIN, MAX, IF, IFERROR, AND, OR, ABS, ROUND, LEN, LEFT, RIGHT, TRIM, CONCAT, CONCATENATE, TEXT, XLOOKUP, VLOOKUP, INDEX, MATCH, TODAY, DATE, YEAR, MONTH, and DAY. Anything else shows #NAME?.
  • Fonts, colors, borders, alignment, number formats, and conditional formatting (highlights, color scales, data bars, and three icon sets).
  • AutoFilter by value, a one-column sort, and find and replace on the current sheet.
  • Pictures you can move and resize.
  • Several sheets: add, rename, delete, switch. Print preview for the selection, used range, sheet, or workbook.

What 1.0 leaves out

  • Spark Grid is not Excel or Numbers. No pivot tables, data-validation lists, comments, or live collaboration.
  • No SUMIF, COUNTIF, SUMIFS, TEXTJOIN, or the rest of the functions missing from the list above.
  • Charts are not in the Mac App Store build. Not on the sheet, and not in a side panel.
  • Defined names already in an Excel file are not imported. Names you create in Spark Grid do work, and they export.
  • The fill handle copies. It does not continue 1, 2, 3 or a series of dates.
  • New and Open replace the current workbook without asking. Quit does ask. Save before you open something else.

Coming next

None of this is in the Mac App Store build.

Coming next, and not in the Mac App Store build: a save prompt before replacing the open file, SUMIF and the functions beside it, defined names and number formats from the file, a series fill, sort and condition filters, a name box, and charts on the sheet with drag to move, resize, custom series and point colors, and the usual title, legend, axis, and gridline controls.

  • New, Open, and a Finder open ask before they replace the file. Quit already asks.
  • SUMIF, AVERAGEIF, COUNTIF, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIFS, COUNTBLANK, SUMPRODUCT, IFNA, IFS, NOT, UPPER, LOWER, MID, SUBSTITUTE, TEXTJOIN, ROUNDUP, ROUNDDOWN, and NOW, with wildcards on the criteria that take them.
  • Defined names already in an Excel file are read on import.
  • Number formats keep the file’s format code, including thousands separators, the currency symbol in that code, and the date and time pattern.
  • Charts on the sheet. Drag to move, resize, custom series and point colors, and the usual title, legend, axis, and gridline controls. A simple Excel bar, line, or area chart can come in with the file. Pie, scatter, and multi-series are not part of this.
  • The fill handle continues numbers and dates. Formulas still shift.
  • The column menu can sort, and filter by greater than, less than, or text contains, beside the value checklist.
  • A name box that takes an address or a defined name, F2 to edit, and ⌘-arrow to the edge of the data.
