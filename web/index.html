Spark Grid

A native Mac spreadsheet for .xlsx and CSV

Open, edit, and save .xlsx and CSV on your Mac. Everyday formulas, filters, formatting, and pictures. No account, and the app does not use the network.

Free on the Mac App Store. Spark Grid 1.0 runs on macOS 14 or later. What this version includes.

macOS 14 or later · Built by Sam Parker · Part of the Spark Suite

Open, edit, and save .xlsx and CSV

For a workbook you already have. Version 1.0 is a finished app with a short formula list and a few hard limits.

  • .xlsx and CSV Open and save both on your Mac. Autosave writes a file you have already saved. The 1.0 list.
  • Everyday formulas SUM, AVERAGE, IF, XLOOKUP, VLOOKUP, INDEX, and MATCH, plus a short set of text and date functions. Any other name shows #NAME?.
  • Filters and formatting Freeze panes, merge, filters, sort, find and replace, conditional formatting, and pictures.

More than one sheet

Add, rename, and switch sheets. Freeze panes stay put, and conditional formatting can mark a column.

Coming next
