Open, edit, and save .xlsx and CSV

For a workbook you already have. Version 1.1.0 opens large workbooks faster, and a formula cell shows its result.

  • .xlsx and CSV Open and save both on your Mac. Autosave writes a file you have already saved. What’s in 1.1.0.
  • Everyday formulas SUM, IF, SUMIF, XLOOKUP, and VLOOKUP, plus text and date functions. The 1.1.0 list. Any other name shows #NAME?.
  • Filters and formatting Freeze panes, merge, filters, sort, find and replace, conditional formatting, pictures, and bar, line, and area charts on the sheet.

More than one sheet

Add, rename, and switch sheets. Freeze panes stay put, and conditional formatting can mark a column.
